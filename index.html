Our Core Philosophy

A return to raw textures, earthy heat, and genuine origin.

We source only single-origin teas and clean herbal elements from small family farms that emphasize soil health and sustainable harvesting. No synthetic flavorings, industrial processing, or hidden fillers enter our workspace.

Each harvest arrives in its raw, unfiltered state. We dry and roast our botanicals inside our clay ovens to capture the pure essence of charcoal heat, mineral soils, and the clean scent of woodsmoke.
