Our Methodology

A slow, deliberate extraction process designed to respect the leaf.

01

Precise Water Chemistry

We heat natural spring water to exact temperatures tailored to each plant's structural threshold. Delicate green leaves are never scolded, while hearty, wood-like barks receive a long, active decoction to bring forth hidden minerality.

02

Vessel Pairing

The clay of the brewing vessel is as important as the water. We steep aged dark teas in porous Yixing purple clay pots that absorb oils and soften tannins over years of use, while using non-porous glass and glaze for bright, floral herbal teas.

03

The Slow Pour

Every tea is poured with full attention to the flow rate, minimizing turbulence in the pot. This traditional approach yields clean extractions without bitterness, preserving the complex aromatic volatile oils of the botanical blend.
